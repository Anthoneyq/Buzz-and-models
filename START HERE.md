# START HERE

Dan, this turns your MacBook into a private AI workstation. The AI "brains" live on your Mac, so there are no monthly AI bills.

## Before you begin

- Plug in the charger. Keep the lid open.
- Shut down Windows and quit Parallels.
- Open **Terminal** (press Command + Space, type "Terminal", press Return).

## Three steps, one at a time

For each step: open the file, copy **everything** in it, paste it into Terminal, press Return. Wait for the words **STEP COMPLETE** before starting the next one.

**Step 01 – Install everything** (about 15 minutes)
Puts the programs on your Mac. It asks for your Mac password once. Nothing appears while you type. That is normal.

**Step 02 – Download the AI models** (a few hours)
Downloads seven AI brains, from a small quick one to a big coding expert. Let it run. If it stops, paste it again and it continues.

**Step 03 – Start and test** (about 15 minutes)
Turns everything on, checks each brain, and opens Buzz.

## What the pieces are

- **Buzz** – the app you work in.
- **oMLX** – the engine that runs the AI brains. It sits quietly at the top of your screen.
- **Models** – the brains themselves.

## Every day after

- Double-click **Start Dan AI** on your Desktop.
- Before opening Windows, double-click **Stop Dan AI**.
- **Dan AI Notes** on your Desktop shows how to connect Buzz.
